# CREDITS — La Última Noche

Juego original: diseño, programación, guion, música procedural y diseño sonoro creados para este proyecto.
Toda la música se sintetiza en tiempo real con Web Audio (guitarra Karplus-Strong, marimba, cajita de música, campanas, cuerdas, percusión). No se usa música comercial.

Notes: Poly Haven models were converted from glTF (1k textures) to single GLB with glTF-Transform; some were split into single objects, simplified, and had textures downsized to 512 px. Poly Pizza models were rescaled to real-world metres and recentred; animation clip names were cleaned (armature prefixes removed). The license of every Poly Pizza model was checked on its model page (CC0 1.0 or CC-BY 3.0); all Poly Haven and ambientCG assets are CC0.


All files in `public/assets/audio/` (MP3, 44.1 kHz). Every source was checked on its own page: only CC0 1.0 or CC-BY licenses are used (in practice all sources are **CC0 1.0 / public domain** - no attribution legally required, credited anyway). Files were trimmed, converted to mono (loops may be stereo), level-normalized, faded/crossfaded for seamless looping, and in some cases filtered/pitched (noted in *Use in game*). Freesound files were taken from the public HQ preview MP3 of each sound.
